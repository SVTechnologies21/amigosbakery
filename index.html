Our Story

Baked with passion, served with love

Sweet Layers began in 2016 in a tiny kitchen in Nellore, where our founder Jayaprakash Rao spent years perfecting the art of cake-making — one layer at a time.

Today, we're a beloved fixture of Nellore's celebrations. Every cake is made to order with locally sourced ingredients, zero preservatives, and an absurd amount of love.
